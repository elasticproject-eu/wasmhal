# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. It applies to everyone who takes part in the project, in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Our standards

Everyone is expected to:

- be respectful and welcoming, whatever a person's background, identity or level of experience;
- give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, focusing on the work rather than the person;
- take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.

The following is not acceptable:

- harassment, insults, or derogatory or discriminatory comments;
- sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention;
- publishing someone else's private information without their explicit permission;
- trolling, or deliberately disrupting discussions;
- any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please report it **privately to the project maintainers through GitHub**. Use the contact details on a maintainer's GitHub profile, or the repository's private reporting form ([Security → Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/elasticproject-eu/wasmhal/security/advisories/new)) and mark the report as a code-of-conduct matter.

Please do not report conduct problems in public issues. All reports are handled confidentially. The maintainers will not act against anyone for reporting in good faith.

## Enforcement

The maintainers are responsible for enforcing this code of conduct. They may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that violate it. Depending on how severe the violation is, they may respond with a correction, a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban, as described in the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
